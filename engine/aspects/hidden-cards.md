# Hidden Cards

## Goal

Capture the legality and effects of face-down play without mixing in too many other rule dimensions.

## Scenario Seeds

- A player cannot hide a card in the first round.
- A player can hide a card from the second round onward.
- A visible card beats a hidden card.
- Two hidden cards in the same round produce a tie.
- A hidden card remains hidden in the public state after it is played.
