# Hand Resolution

## Goal

Capture how a hand ends across its three rounds, including ties and early resolution.

## Scenario Seeds

- One player wins the first two rounds and the hand ends immediately.
- Each player wins one round and the third round decides the hand.
- One player wins one round and another round is tied, and the non-tied round decides the hand.
- The first round is tied, then one player wins the second round and the hand ends without needing a third round.
- The first round is tied, the second round is tied, and the third round decides the hand.
- All three rounds are tied and the player who led the first round wins the hand.
- Both players have a winning round and the hand outcome is determined by the first non-tied round when the sequence allows it.
- Mirror the same hand-ending cases from both starting players' perspectives.
- A hand that is already decided must not accept additional card plays.
